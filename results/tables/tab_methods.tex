\caption{The 31 methods by family (the grouping of the repository's registry). Kind: non-LLM, LLM (asks a language model), or mixed (a combination of both). Similarity: the similarity tables the method reads (fallback: only when the model's answer is unusable). Members: the methods a combined method runs, each at its own tuned settings. Tuned settings: the values chosen on the 60 tuning lectures; fwd and back are the shared costs per slide moved forward and back, stay the reward for staying on a slide from one unit to the next, prior the weight of the positional prior (Section~\ref{sec:methods}); the others are the method's own. Combinations: the setting combinations in the method's tuning grid, times the eight measures where the method tunes its measure; they add up to the 25{,}773 combinations of Section~\ref{sec:tuning}.}
